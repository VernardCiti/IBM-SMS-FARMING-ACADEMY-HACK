watsonx.ai SMS Farming Academy

IBM Hackathon Entry: Sustainable Agriculture Training Platform

This platform provides SMS-based agricultural training to farmers in regions with limited internet connectivity, focusing on sustainable practices and resource efficiency.

Instructions:

  • Send "farm" to start your training journey
  • Answer quiz questions with "a", "b", or "c"
  • Text "certificate" after completing all lessons
  • Try "help" for assistance or "topics" for available lessons
  • Toggle connection status to simulate different network conditions

IBM watsonx.ai Integration

This simulator connects to IBM watsonx.ai using the Granite 13B foundation model:

  • WATSONX_API_KEY - For accessing IBM Granite 13B model
  • PROJECT_ID - For IBM Cloud project access
  • TOKEN_COST - For usage tracking (default: 0.0001 USD/token)

SDG Alignment: This solution addresses Sustainable Development Goals 8.2 (higher economic productivity through technology) and 8.4 (improving resource efficiency in consumption and production).

Blockchain Certificate: Farmers receive a verifiable IBM Blockchain credential upon completion, which serves as proof of skills for potential employers or farming cooperatives.

Note: This simulator makes real API calls to IBM watsonx.ai for dynamic content generation. No actual SMS are sent.
